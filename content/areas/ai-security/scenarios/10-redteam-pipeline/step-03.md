## Run the battery (secure)

Run the same suite in secure mode. Every module's control engages, and the ASR
collapses.

The **Check** confirms the reduction: secure-mode ASR drops to a third or less.
That delta — high in vulnerable, low in secure — is your **risk-reduction
number**, computed from real attacks rather than asserted.

This is exactly the table the prompt pack calls for: attack-success rate before
vs after, across every module, reproducible on demand.
