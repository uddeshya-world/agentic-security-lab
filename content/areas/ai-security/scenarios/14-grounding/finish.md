## What you proved

- An agent can state a fabricated policy in the same confident voice as a true
  one; users can't tell the difference (**LLM09**).
- **C19** verifies *support*, not truth: unsupported claims are withheld,
  supported claims carry `[source N]` citations.
- Citation is also an anti-poisoning control — it forces a poisoned source into
  view instead of letting the agent's authority launder it.
- Stated honestly: this does not catch a legitimately-retrieved source that is
  simply wrong. That's data quality, and it's a different control.
