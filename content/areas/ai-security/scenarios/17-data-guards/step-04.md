## Content safety is a different product

Toxicity, hate, sexual content, "denied topics" — that is **content safety**
(Llama Guard, Azure harm categories). It is not DLP.

DLP asks: *is this someone's SSN?*  
Content safety asks: *is this harmful speech?*

The measured-guardrails lab later reports catch rate on both kinds of probe.
Do not mix the labels on a design review.
