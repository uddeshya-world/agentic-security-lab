## Five requirements

1. **An approved list of recipients and websites** for anything the agent sends, with
   a named owner who approves additions. *Ask for AISVS v1.0 C7.3.3 (L2).*
2. **A limit on how many records one request can read**, and a log of every read.
   *Ask for AISVS v1.0 C9.5.1 (L2) and AISVS v1.0 C12.4.2 (L2).*
3. **Human approval for named high-impact actions**, with an estimate of how many
   approvals a day that person will handle. *Ask for AISVS v1.0 C9.2.1 (L1) and AISVS v1.0 C9.2.2 (L2).*
4. **Pinned third-party tools**: each tool's description is reviewed and locked, and a
   change needs review again. *Ask for AISVS v1.0 C10.4.8 (L3), AISVS v1.0 C9.3.3 (L2) and AISVS v1.0 C9.3.4 (L2).*
5. **Proof, not promises**: for each control, a test that runs the attack and shows it
   stopped. A sentence in the model's instructions is not a control.

The ids are OWASP AISVS v1.0 requirements (L1 to L3). Citing one in a tender tells the vendor
what you will verify. It does not make anything compliant.
