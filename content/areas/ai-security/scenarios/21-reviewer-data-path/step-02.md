## Where the control goes

There are usually many ways in and few ways out. Filters on the way in are a race:
the attacker rephrases. Controls on the way out (an approved recipient list, an
approved website list) behave the same way every time and cover every way in at once.

When several agents share storage, check the combined path, not each agent. Three
agents can each pass review alone and still leak together. Ask for the control at the
point where private data would leave, wherever it came from.
