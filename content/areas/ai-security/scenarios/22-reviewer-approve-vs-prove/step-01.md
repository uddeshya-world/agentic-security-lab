## Described is not demonstrated

Approval documents often list controls: "input filtering", "guardrails", "human in the
loop". Each of those can be real or can be a slide.

A demonstrated control comes with a run: the attack was attempted, the record shows
where it stopped, and the same test runs again whenever the system changes. The full
CyberRange lab grades exactly this: a check passes only on evidence a run left behind.
