## The five questions

Every triage answers these, in this order, and stops guessing at the first one it
cannot support with evidence:

1. **What was taken?** Records, fields, count.
2. **Where did it go?** Destination, and whether you control that destination.
3. **When?** First and last observed activity.
4. **How?** The path — which capability was abused, in what order.
5. **Is it still happening?** The only one that changes what you do in the next
   five minutes.

Hold that list. You will fill it from the event log and the sink, not from what
you remember doing.
