## The measure half

Attack, defend, measure. The third one is where most write-ups quietly stop.

The measurement has to be a **controlled comparison**: same payload, same path,
one variable changed. If you also change the query, or the recipient, or the
mode of a second tool, you have proved nothing about the control — you have
produced a different experiment with a different result.

So: identical exfiltration, secure mode, and read the outcome.
