## Re-run with the control on

Set the mode switch to **secure** and press **Run**. Same chain, same payload.

The trace stops short of WORLD and the caption names the control that stopped it.
That naming matters more than the block: *blocked* tells you it failed, *which
control blocked it* tells you what to keep funding, and which layer would still
be exposed if that one were bypassed.

Read the `DEFENSE` line in the timeline before you check.
