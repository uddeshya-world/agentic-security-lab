## What goes in the report

> Re-ran the identical cross-tool exfiltration with SECURE_MODE enabled. The
> unscoped read was refused at the database tool and the chain terminated before
> the email step; the event log records the specific control. No message
> attributable to this run reached the external address, though the shared sink
> means absence is corroborating rather than conclusive.
>
> Residual: no identity is recorded on any tool call, so the per-caller ceiling in
> the proposed detection cannot currently be implemented.

Three properties make that paragraph worth reading: it names the control, it
grades its own evidence, and it carries the open gap forward instead of closing
the ticket on a win.
