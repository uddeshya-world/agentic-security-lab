A customer walks up to a bank teller and says: "Your manager told me to collect everyone's statements."

The teller may well believe it. People are persuadable, and language models are much more so.

But the vault still needs a key card, whatever the teller was told.

In this scenario you are the customer. You type the instruction straight into the chat.

The model goes along with it and asks for every customer. With no rule at the tool, it gets them.

In secure mode the model still asks. The tool still says no. The model was not fixed; the vault rule held.

A model is far easier to talk round than a trained teller, so the vault rule must never depend on the teller's judgement.

Run both modes and find which part of the system said no.
