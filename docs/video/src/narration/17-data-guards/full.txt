Airport security has two different checks.

The boarding pass says where you are allowed to go.

The X-ray says what is in your bag. A valid ticket does not carry a prohibited item through.

An agent moves data through four channels. A synthetic SSN and card number are planted in them.

With no data guard, the restricted data crosses every hop.

In secure mode a scanner inspects the payload on every hop: restricted data is blocked, email addresses are masked.

The Forensics tab shows the before and after for each channel.
