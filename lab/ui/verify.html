badge verification

Check what a badge actually says

Paste a badge token. This page checks the signature against this instance's key and then shows you the part that matters: the transcript of graded checks the holder passed, and the mode each one was graded in.

A valid signature proves one thing: the transcript has not been edited since this instance issued it, and every check in it was recorded by this instance's server when the check passed. It does not prove who sat at the keyboard, and it is not third-party attestation, because whoever runs the lab also holds the key.

Local only. All names, records and domains are synthetic.
